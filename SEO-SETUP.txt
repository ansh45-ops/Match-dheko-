IMPORTANT — COMPLETE THESE BEFORE LAUNCH:
1. Set the real public website domain. Add a canonical URL in <head>:
   <link rel="canonical" href="https://YOUR-REAL-DOMAIN/">
2. Replace the sitemap.xml placeholder with the real homepage URL.
3. Add the real sitemap URL to robots.txt.
4. Create a genuine 1200x630 social sharing image and add og:image and
   twitter:image tags. Do not use a logo or artwork you do not own.
5. Verify the domain in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools;
   submit sitemap.xml and inspect the homepage.
6. Ensure the site is publicly accessible over HTTPS, returns HTTP 200,
   and is not blocked by hosting-level robots/noindex settings.
7. Keep page text truthful. Do not claim every match, every channel, or
   guaranteed free access. Search engines may ignore meta keywords and
   structured data does not guarantee rich results or rankings.

SEO cannot guarantee #1 rankings or instant indexing. Rankings depend on
the real domain, useful original content, reputation, technical performance,
links, competition, and ongoing updates. Do not publish misleading claims
such as "all cricket free" if broadcast rights/access do not support them.
